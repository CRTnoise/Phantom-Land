03 — discovery

Finding peers, with no server.

Your room's code becomes a topic announced to a public distributed network (a DHT). It only helps peers find each other — it never sees a single message.

04 — connection

Punching straight through.

Both of you sit behind home routers. Phantom coordinates a direct hole-punch so your machines connect to each other — no relay in the middle.

05 — key exchange

Two halves, one secret.

An X25519 exchange: you each combine your private key with the other's public key and arrive at the same shared secret — without it ever crossing the wire.

06 — the message

Watch it vanish into cipher.

Type a message. It's encrypted on your device, travels as nothing but noise, and is only readable on the other end.
